# Style 07: Quiet Luxury

## Prompt block

Quiet luxury look. Cream, warm white, taupe and greige with walnut, warm stone, travertine and selective marble, fine linen, wool and boucle. Fewer high-quality pieces in generous proportions, soft warm light. Expensive through material and restraint, not decoration.

## Mood

Timeless, sophisticated, understated.

## Palette

Cream, warm white, taupe, greige, walnut, espresso and brushed bronze accents.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: large proportionate sofa, sculptural lounge chair, stone coffee table, wool rug, subtle lamps, full-height heavy linen curtains.
- Dining: solid walnut table, upholstered chairs.
- Bedroom: upholstered bed, walnut bedside tables, wool rug, full-height layered linen curtains.
- Study: walnut desk, leather chair, brushed bronze task lamp.
- Kitchen: a marble board, a stone bowl, one branch in a vase.
- Bathroom: cream towels, a marble tray, a small orchid.
- Balcony: teak lounge chair with cream cushions, stone side table, large planter.

## Textiles and window treatments

Heavy linen, wool, boucle, sparing velvet. Full-height layered curtains.

## Lighting

Soft warm light, subtle lamps. Recessed light only at existing points.

## Greenery and decor

Large understated artwork, sculptural ceramics, stone objects, premium books.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Warm white or greige paint, or subtle limewash.
- Flooring: Wide walnut or oak, or large-format honed stone. Honed stone in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: Elegant pendants or soft recessed light at existing light points.
- Carpentry: Walnut or greige built-ins with brushed bronze handles.
- Kitchen: walnut or greige fronts, marble or travertine top and slab backsplash, brushed bronze handles.
- Bathroom: honed marble or travertine, walnut vanity, brushed bronze tapware.

## Avoid

Flashy gold, crystal chandeliers, logos, hotel-lobby styling, excess marble.

## Marketing fit

Affluent professionals, premium condos.
