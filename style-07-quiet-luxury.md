# Style 07: Quiet Luxury

## Prompt block

Quiet luxury style: cream, warm white, taupe and greige with walnut and warm stone, travertine and selective marble, fine linen, wool and boucle. Fewer high-quality pieces: a large proportionate sofa, a sculptural lounge chair, a stone coffee table. Full-height heavy linen curtains and soft warm architectural light. Expensive through material and restraint, not decoration.

## Mood

Timeless, sophisticated, understated.

## Palette

Cream, warm white, taupe, greige, walnut, espresso and brushed bronze accents.

## Furniture

Large proportionate sofa, sculptural lounge chair, stone coffee table, upholstered bed, solid timber pieces.

## Textiles and window treatments

Heavy linen, wool, boucle, sparing velvet. Full-height layered curtains.

## Lighting

Soft warm recessed light in existing points, elegant pendant, subtle lamps.

## Greenery and decor

Large understated artwork, sculptural ceramics, stone objects, premium books.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Warm white or greige paint, or subtle limewash.
- Flooring: Wide walnut or oak, or large-format honed stone.
- Kitchen: walnut or greige fronts, marble or travertine top and slab backsplash, brushed bronze handles.
- Bathroom: honed marble or travertine, walnut vanity, brushed bronze tapware.

## Avoid

Flashy gold, crystal chandeliers, logos, hotel-lobby styling, excess marble.

## Marketing fit

Affluent professionals, premium condos.
