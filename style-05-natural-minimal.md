# Style 05: Natural Minimal

## Prompt block

Natural minimal look, often called Muji style in Singapore. Light and practical: off-white, light ash or birch timber, oatmeal and grey linen and cotton, simple rectangular forms, neat lidded boxes, one potted plant, warm white light. Clean, functional, orderly, nothing ornamental.

## Mood

Light, practical, orderly, calm.

## Palette

White, off-white, light ash timber, oatmeal, light grey.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: simple sofa with a removable oatmeal cover, light ash coffee table, open ash shelf with neat boxes, low-pile cotton rug, simple white curtains.
- Dining: rectangular ash table with a bench and chairs.
- Bedroom: light timber platform bed, grey and oatmeal linen bedding, ash bedside table, simple oat curtains.
- Study: ash desk, simple chair, open shelf with neat boxes.
- Kitchen: clear jars, a timber board, a small potted herb.
- Bathroom: white and grey towels, a timber tray.
- Balcony: folding timber chair, one potted plant.

## Textiles and window treatments

Oatmeal and grey linen, cotton, simple white or oat curtains.

## Lighting

White paper or fabric shades, warm white light.

## Greenery and decor

One potted plant, neat storage boxes, books.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: White or off-white matte paint.
- Flooring: Light ash or birch-look vinyl. White or light grey tiles in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: White paper or fabric pendants at existing light points.
- Carpentry: Light timber-laminate or white built-ins with simple pulls.
- Kitchen: light timber-laminate or white fronts, finger pulls or simple bar handles, white or light grey top, white subway tile.
- Bathroom: white tiles, timber-look vanity, white fittings, chrome tapware.

## Avoid

Dark colours, glossy luxury finishes, ornate items, clutter.

## Marketing fit

HDB owners, first-time buyers, young couples, practical family units.
