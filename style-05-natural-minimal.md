# Style 05: Natural Minimal

## Prompt block

Natural minimal style, often called Muji style in Singapore: light and practical, white walls, light ash or birch timber, oatmeal and grey linen, simple rectangular furniture, open shelving with neat storage boxes, low-pile cotton rug, white paper pendant, one potted plant. Clean, functional, orderly, nothing ornamental.

## Mood

Light, practical, orderly, calm.

## Palette

White, off-white, light ash timber, oatmeal, light grey.

## Furniture

Light timber platform bed, simple sofa with a removable cover, open shelving, rectangular dining table with a bench.

## Textiles and window treatments

Oatmeal and grey linen, cotton, simple white or oat curtains.

## Lighting

White paper or fabric pendant, warm white light.

## Greenery and decor

One potted plant, neat storage boxes, books.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: White or off-white matte paint.
- Flooring: Light ash or birch-look vinyl. White or light grey tiles in wet areas.
- Kitchen: light timber-laminate or white fronts, finger pulls or simple bar handles, white or light grey top, white subway tile.
- Bathroom: white tiles, timber-look vanity, white fittings, chrome tapware.

## Avoid

Dark colours, glossy luxury finishes, ornate items, clutter.

## Marketing fit

HDB owners, first-time buyers, young couples, practical family units.
