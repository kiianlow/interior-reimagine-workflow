# Style 11: Mid-Century Modern

## Prompt block

Mid-century modern look. Walnut and teak on tapered legs, mustard, olive and rust fabrics against warm white, geometric patterns, globe and mushroom lamp shapes, graphic abstract art and a few retro ceramics. Warm, playful and tidy.

## Mood

Warm, playful, tidy, a little retro.

## Palette

Warm white, walnut, teak, mustard, olive, rust.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: low sleek sofa in mustard, olive or rust on tapered legs, walnut coffee table, geometric rug, mushroom lamp, simple curtains.
- Dining: round walnut table, shell-style chairs.
- Bedroom: walnut bed on tapered legs, solid-colour bedding, geometric rug, simple curtains.
- Study: walnut desk on tapered legs, shell chair, mushroom lamp.
- Kitchen: retro ceramic jars, a walnut board.
- Bathroom: mustard towels, a round tray.
- Balcony: shell-style chairs, small round table, one plant.

## Textiles and window treatments

Geometric rug, solid-colour cushions, simple curtains.

## Lighting

Mushroom table lamps, warm light. Globe pendants only in REVAMP, at existing light points.

## Greenery and decor

Graphic art, retro ceramics, one or two plants.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Warm white paint, optional one muted accent wall in olive or terracotta.
- Flooring: Walnut-look vinyl, herringbone timber look or terrazzo. Terrazzo or hex tiles in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: Globe pendants at existing light points.
- Carpentry: Walnut-look built-ins with slim brass handles.
- Kitchen: walnut-look fronts with slim timber or brass handles, white or terrazzo top, optional coloured tile.
- Bathroom: terrazzo or hex tiles, walnut vanity, round mirror.

## Avoid

Colour on every surface, kitsch props, clutter.

## Marketing fit

Young couples, creatives, character resale HDB units.
