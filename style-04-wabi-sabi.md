# Style 04: Wabi-Sabi

## Prompt block

Earthy, tactile wabi-sabi look. Warm off-white, sand, clay and muted olive. Raw oak and weathered timber, handmade ceramics, stone accents, crumpled natural linen, low rounded organic forms, one dried branch arrangement, soft warm low light. Quiet, imperfect and lived-in, never cluttered.

## Mood

Earthy, tactile, quiet, beautifully imperfect.

## Palette

Warm off-white, sand, clay, muted olive, soft charcoal.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: low sofa with a natural linen slipcover, chunky timber coffee table, stone side table, undyed wool rug, ceramic table lamp, linen curtains.
- Dining: chunky raw oak table, simple timber chairs or a bench, a handmade bowl.
- Bedroom: low timber bed with crumpled linen bedding, timber bedside stool, linen curtains.
- Study: raw oak desk, woven chair, ceramic lamp.
- Kitchen: handmade ceramic jars, a raw timber board, dried grasses.
- Bathroom: undyed linen towels, a stone dish, one dried stem.
- Balcony: low timber stool, large clay pot with an olive tree or grasses.

## Textiles and window treatments

Crumpled linen, cotton and wool in undyed tones. Linen curtains.

## Lighting

Paper lanterns and ceramic lamps, warm and low.

## Greenery and decor

Dried branches or grasses, handmade ceramics, stones, a few books.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Limewash, textured plaster or warm sand microcement finish.
- Flooring: Warm microcement look, terracotta-toned tiles or wide matte oak. Terracotta or stone-look tiles in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: Paper lantern pendants at existing light points.
- Carpentry: Raw oak or plaster-look built-ins.
- Kitchen: raw oak or plaster-look fronts, honed travertine or stone top, handmade-look warm white tile.
- Bathroom: microcement-look walls, stone vessel basin, travertine, aged brass or matte tapware.

## Avoid

Gloss, chrome, perfect symmetry, bright colour, synthetic finishes.

## Marketing fit

Design-led buyers, lifestyle and RedNote content.
