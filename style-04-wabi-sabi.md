# Style 04: Wabi-Sabi

## Prompt block

Wabi-sabi style: earthy and tactile, warm off-white, sand, clay and muted olive. Limewash or textured plaster walls, raw oak and weathered timber, handmade ceramics, stone accents, natural linen, low rounded furniture with organic shapes, soft warm light, one dried branch arrangement. Quiet, imperfect and lived-in, never cluttered.

## Mood

Earthy, tactile, quiet, beautifully imperfect.

## Palette

Warm off-white, sand, clay, muted olive, soft charcoal.

## Furniture

Low sofa with a natural linen slipcover, chunky timber coffee table, stone side tables, rounded organic forms.

## Textiles and window treatments

Crumpled linen, cotton and wool in undyed tones. Linen curtains.

## Lighting

Paper lanterns and ceramic lamps, warm and low.

## Greenery and decor

Dried branches or grasses, handmade ceramics, stones, a few books.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Limewash, textured plaster or warm sand microcement finish.
- Flooring: Warm microcement look, terracotta-toned tiles or wide matte oak.
- Kitchen: raw oak or plaster-look fronts, honed travertine or stone top, handmade-look warm white tile.
- Bathroom: microcement-look walls, stone vessel basin, travertine, aged brass or matte tapware.

## Avoid

Gloss, chrome, perfect symmetry, bright colour, synthetic finishes.

## Marketing fit

Design-led buyers, lifestyle and RedNote content.
