# Style 10: Modern Tropical

## Prompt block

Modern tropical style for a Singapore home: warm white, sand and beige with teak, selective rattan and cane, travertine or limestone, woven textures, deep green and olive accents, comfortable low lounge furniture, linen curtains, large leafy plants. Relaxed and refined, a tropical residence rather than a resort.

## Mood

Warm, green, relaxed, refined.

## Palette

Warm white, sand, beige, teak, deep green, olive, muted terracotta.

## Furniture

Comfortable low sofa, teak coffee table, cane or rattan accent chair, timber dining set.

## Textiles and window treatments

Linen curtains, woven rugs, cotton cushions in green and sand.

## Lighting

Warm daylight, woven pendants. A ceiling fan if the ceiling allows.

## Greenery and decor

Large leafy plants such as bird of paradise or monstera, woven baskets, ceramics.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Warm white or sand paint, textured plaster.
- Flooring: Teak-look timber, terrazzo or warm stone tiles.
- Kitchen: teak or oak fronts with cane inserts, terrazzo or stone top, optional green tile backsplash.
- Bathroom: terrazzo, teak accents, stone basin.

## Avoid

Resort kitsch, bamboo overload, heavy dark interiors.

## Marketing fit

Landed homes, ground-floor PES units, balcony units.
