# Style 10: Modern Tropical

## Prompt block

Modern tropical look for a Singapore home. Warm white, sand and beige with teak, selective rattan and cane, travertine or limestone, woven textures, deep green and olive accents, large leafy plants. Relaxed and refined, a tropical residence rather than a resort.

## Mood

Warm, green, relaxed, refined.

## Palette

Warm white, sand, beige, teak, deep green, olive, muted terracotta.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: comfortable low sofa, teak coffee table, cane or rattan accent chair, woven rug, linen curtains, large monstera or bird of paradise.
- Dining: teak table, cane chairs.
- Bedroom: teak bed with a cane headboard, woven rug, linen curtains, one leafy plant.
- Study: teak desk, rattan chair, woven lamp.
- Kitchen: a woven basket, a teak board, potted herbs.
- Bathroom: sand towels, a teak tray, a small fern.
- Balcony: teak lounge chairs, woven side table, large leafy plants in planters.

## Textiles and window treatments

Linen curtains, woven rugs, cotton cushions in green and sand.

## Lighting

Warm daylight, woven lamps. A ceiling fan only in REVAMP, at an existing light point.

## Greenery and decor

Large leafy plants such as bird of paradise or monstera, woven baskets, ceramics.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Warm white or sand paint, or textured plaster.
- Flooring: Teak-look timber, terrazzo or warm stone tiles. Terrazzo or stone tiles in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: Woven pendants at existing light points, or a ceiling fan at an existing light point if the ceiling height allows.
- Carpentry: Teak or oak built-ins with cane inserts.
- Kitchen: teak or oak fronts with cane inserts, terrazzo or stone top, optional green tile backsplash.
- Bathroom: terrazzo, teak accents, stone basin.

## Avoid

Resort kitsch, bamboo overload, heavy dark interiors.

## Marketing fit

Landed homes, ground-floor PES units, balcony units.
