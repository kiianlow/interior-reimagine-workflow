# Style 03: Modern Minimalist Monochrome

## Prompt block

Modern minimalist monochrome look. White, grey and black in varied tones with at most one accent colour. Matte and honed materials, smoked or blackened wood, black or brushed metal, leather or solid fabric. Sharp structured pieces with low visual weight, one bold sculptural object, layered warm neutral light. Sparse.

## Mood

Sleek, urban, architectural, quietly confident.

## Palette

White, grey and black in varying tones. No more than one non-neutral accent.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: structured sofa in charcoal fabric or leather, black metal and stone coffee table, solid grey rug, black sculptural floor lamp, grey sheer curtains.
- Dining: stone or black table, black metal chairs.
- Bedroom: low platform bed, solid grey bedding, black metal bedside tables, grey sheers or roller blinds.
- Study: black metal desk, leather chair, black task lamp.
- Kitchen: one black vase and a stone board, nothing else.
- Bathroom: grey towels, a black tray.
- Balcony: black metal chairs, concrete planter with an architectural plant.

## Textiles and window treatments

Solid colours only, no pattern. Structured over slouchy. Grey sheers or roller blinds.

## Lighting

Sculptural black or chrome fixture as the anchor, layered ambient light, neutral warm white.

## Greenery and decor

Optional: one architectural plant in a concrete or black planter. One bold sculptural object.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: White or light grey matte paint. One charcoal paint wall at most.
- Flooring: Large-format matte grey porcelain, concrete-look tiles or light grey vinyl. Matte grey porcelain in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: Black or chrome sculptural fittings at existing light points.
- Carpentry: Handleless matte white or grey built-ins.
- Kitchen: handleless matte white or grey fronts, sintered stone or quartz top in white or concrete grey, black tapware.
- Bathroom: large-format grey tiles, floating vanity, black fittings, frameless glass.

## Avoid

Pattern, rustic timber, many small objects, clutter.

## Marketing fit

Young professionals, urban condos, rental units.
