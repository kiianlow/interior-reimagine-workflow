# Style 01: Scandinavian

## Prompt block

Bright and airy Scandinavian look. Warm off-white, light beige and soft grey with one muted accent of dusty blue or sage. Light oak, boucle, linen, jute and wool, a chunky knit throw, simple ceramics, warm white light. A few well-chosen pieces, uncluttered.

## Mood

Bright, airy, effortless, Nordic calm.

## Palette

White, soft warm grey, warm off-white, light beige. One muted accent: dusty blue, sage or soft mustard.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: low sofa in light fabric, rounded oak coffee table, jute or wool rug, sculptural floor lamp, olive tree in a ceramic pot, sheer white linen curtains.
- Dining: simple oak table, oak chairs with woven seats, a ceramic vase.
- Bedroom: bed with a linen headboard, oak bedside tables, wool rug, knit throw, sheer white linen curtains.
- Study: oak desk, light fabric chair, slim oak shelf, table lamp.
- Kitchen: white ceramic jars, an oak board, a small herb pot.
- Bathroom: white towels, a light oak tray, a small green plant.
- Balcony: oak or teak lounge chair, small side table, potted olive tree.

## Textiles and window treatments

Chunky knit throws, plain linen cushions, woven jute or wool rug, sheer white linen curtains.

## Lighting

Maximise daylight. One sculptural floor or table lamp, warm white light. Pendants only in REVAMP, at existing light points.

## Greenery and decor

One or two structural plants such as a fiddle leaf fig or olive tree in simple ceramic or woven pots. Minimal line art, one or two ceramics.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: White or soft warm grey matte paint.
- Flooring: Light oak-look SPC vinyl or light engineered timber. Pale matte porcelain in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: White dome or paper pendants at existing light points.
- Carpentry: White or light oak flat-front built-ins.
- Kitchen: white or pale grey matte flat fronts with light oak accents, slim black or brushed handles, white quartz top, white or pale grey tile backsplash.
- Bathroom: white matte tiles, light oak vanity, white basin, matte black or chrome tapware, clear glass screen.

## Avoid

Dark heavy timber, clutter, ornate pieces, fur rugs, cold blue light.

## Marketing fit

Young families, HDB upgraders, bright compact units.
