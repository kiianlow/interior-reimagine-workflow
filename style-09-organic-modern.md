# Style 09: Organic Modern

## Prompt block

Organic modern style: warm white, sand and oat with light to mid oak, travertine and clay tones, curved sofa and rounded furniture, boucle and textured linen, stone and ceramic accents, an arched mirror, sheer curtains, warm natural light, one large sculptural plant.

## Mood

Soft, natural, contemporary, relaxed.

## Palette

Warm white, sand, oat, clay, light to mid oak.

## Furniture

Curved sofa, rounded coffee table, sculptural chairs, soft-edged bed.

## Textiles and window treatments

Boucle, textured linen, sheer curtains, soft wool rug.

## Lighting

Warm natural light, ceramic or paper lamps.

## Greenery and decor

One large sculptural plant, ceramics, stone objects. Arches only in mirrors and furniture, never as new openings.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Warm white paint or soft limewash.
- Flooring: Light to mid oak, or warm stone-look tiles.
- Kitchen: oak fronts, travertine or warm quartz top, fluted details.
- Bathroom: warm stone tiles, round mirror, oak vanity, brushed nickel tapware.

## Avoid

New arched openings, clutter, hard sharp edges everywhere.

## Marketing fit

Families, larger condos.
