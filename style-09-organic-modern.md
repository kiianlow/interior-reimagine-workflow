# Style 09: Organic Modern

## Prompt block

Organic modern look. Warm white, sand and oat with light to mid oak, travertine and clay tones, curved and rounded shapes, boucle and textured linen, stone and ceramic accents, warm natural light, one large sculptural plant.

## Mood

Soft, natural, contemporary, relaxed.

## Palette

Warm white, sand, oat, clay, light to mid oak.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: curved boucle sofa, rounded coffee table, soft wool rug, arched floor mirror, sheer curtains, one large sculptural plant.
- Dining: round oak table, sculptural chairs.
- Bedroom: soft-edged upholstered bed, round bedside tables, wool rug, sheer curtains.
- Study: oak desk with rounded edges, boucle chair, ceramic lamp.
- Kitchen: ceramic jars, a travertine tray, a small plant.
- Bathroom: oat towels, a round stone tray, a small plant.
- Balcony: rounded outdoor lounge chair, clay planter with a sculptural plant.

## Textiles and window treatments

Boucle, textured linen, sheer curtains, soft wool rug.

## Lighting

Warm natural light, ceramic or paper lamps.

## Greenery and decor

One large sculptural plant, ceramics, stone objects. Arches only in mirrors and furniture, never as new openings.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Warm white paint or soft limewash.
- Flooring: Light to mid oak, or warm stone-look tiles. Warm stone-look tiles in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: Ceramic or paper pendants at existing light points.
- Carpentry: Oak built-ins with rounded edges and fluted details.
- Kitchen: oak fronts, travertine or warm quartz top, fluted details.
- Bathroom: warm stone tiles, round mirror, oak vanity, brushed nickel tapware.

## Avoid

New arched openings, clutter, hard sharp edges everywhere.

## Marketing fit

Families, larger condos.
