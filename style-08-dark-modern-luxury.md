# Style 08: Dark Modern Luxury

## Prompt block

Dark modern luxury style: charcoal, espresso, deep taupe and black with warm walnut, smoked glass, dark veined stone and brushed bronze. Low tailored sofa in deep fabric or leather, sculptural statement pendant, warm indirect lamp light, large abstract art. Moody and sophisticated but bright enough to read the room, never theatrical.

## Mood

Moody, sophisticated, intimate.

## Palette

Charcoal, espresso, deep taupe, black, warm walnut, brushed bronze.

## Furniture

Low tailored sofa, leather lounge chair, dark stone coffee table, upholstered bed with a tall headboard.

## Textiles and window treatments

Deep-toned wool, velvet used sparingly, dark sheers.

## Lighting

Warm indirect lamp light and a statement pendant. No bright cool white light.

## Greenery and decor

Large abstract art, sculptural objects, one dark-leaved plant.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Dark greige or charcoal matte paint. In small or dim rooms, keep walls lighter and use dark furniture instead.
- Flooring: Dark oak or dark stone-look tiles.
- Kitchen: dark matte fronts, dark veined stone top, bronze handles.
- Bathroom: dark stone-look tiles, smoked glass, bronze tapware.

## Avoid

Theatrical lighting, glossy black everywhere, rooms too dark to read.

## Marketing fit

Luxury units, high-floor units with strong daylight, evening hero shots.
