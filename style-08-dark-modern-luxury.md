# Style 08: Dark Modern Luxury

## Prompt block

Dark modern luxury look. Charcoal, espresso, deep taupe and black with warm walnut, smoked glass, dark veined stone and brushed bronze, deep-toned wool and leather, warm indirect lamp light, large abstract art. Moody and sophisticated but bright enough to read the room, never theatrical.

## Mood

Moody, sophisticated, intimate.

## Palette

Charcoal, espresso, deep taupe, black, warm walnut, brushed bronze.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: low tailored sofa in deep fabric or leather, leather lounge chair, dark stone coffee table, deep-toned wool rug, large abstract art, dark sheer curtains.
- Dining: dark stone or walnut table, leather chairs.
- Bedroom: upholstered bed with a tall headboard, walnut bedside tables, dark bedding, dark sheers.
- Study: walnut desk, leather chair, bronze task lamp.
- Kitchen: a dark stone board, a bronze bowl.
- Bathroom: charcoal towels, a smoked glass tray.
- Balcony: dark metal lounge chair, dark planter with a dark-leaved plant.

## Textiles and window treatments

Deep-toned wool, velvet used sparingly, dark sheers.

## Lighting

Warm indirect lamp light. No bright cool white light.

## Greenery and decor

Large abstract art, sculptural objects, one dark-leaved plant.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Dark greige or charcoal matte paint. In HDB flats, small rooms or dim photos, keep walls and ceiling light and use dark furniture instead.
- Flooring: Dark oak or dark stone-look tiles. Dark stone-look tiles in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: One sculptural statement pendant at an existing light point.
- Carpentry: Dark matte or walnut built-ins with bronze handles.
- Kitchen: dark matte fronts, dark veined stone top, bronze handles.
- Bathroom: dark stone-look tiles, smoked glass, bronze tapware.

## Avoid

Theatrical lighting, glossy black everywhere, rooms too dark to read.

## Marketing fit

Luxury units, high-floor units with strong daylight, evening hero shots.
