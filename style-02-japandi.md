# Style 02: Japandi

## Prompt block

Calm, grounded Japandi look. Warm neutrals with soft charcoal, muted clay and taupe. Walnut or dark oak with low horizontal lines, linen and hemp, stoneware, one sculptural branch, soft warm diffused light. Generous negative space, every object intentional.

## Mood

Calm, grounded, quietly disciplined. Japanese restraint with Scandinavian warmth.

## Palette

Warm neutrals, soft charcoal, muted clay, deep taupe.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: low timber-frame sofa in oat linen, low walnut coffee table, low-pile natural rug, paper floor lamp, oat linen curtains.
- Dining: walnut table with simple timber chairs or a bench, a stoneware bowl.
- Bedroom: low platform bed, linen bedding in oat and clay, low bedside tables, low-pile rug, oat linen curtains.
- Study: walnut desk, simple timber chair, paper table lamp.
- Kitchen: stoneware jars, a walnut tray, one branch in a vase.
- Bathroom: oat towels, a stone soap dish, one small plant.
- Balcony: low timber bench, stoneware planter with a small tree.

## Textiles and window treatments

Linen or hemp in oat, clay and charcoal. Low-pile natural rug. Oat linen curtains.

## Lighting

Soft, diffused, warm. Paper or rattan shades. No bright white light.

## Greenery and decor

A single sculptural plant or branch arrangement in a stoneware vessel. A stoneware bowl, a low tray, one abstract ink artwork.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Warm white or greige matte paint, or soft clay limewash.
- Flooring: Mid to dark oak-look vinyl or engineered timber. Matte stone-look tiles in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: Paper or rattan pendants at existing light points.
- Carpentry: Walnut or dark oak veneer built-ins with slatted details.
- Kitchen: walnut or dark oak veneer fronts, handleless or slim recessed pulls, matte warm stone or quartz top, handmade-look warm white tile backsplash.
- Bathroom: warm grey stone-look tiles, timber vanity, matte stone basin, matte black or gunmetal tapware, fluted glass.

## Avoid

Glossy finishes, bright colour, clutter, decoration for its own sake.

## Marketing fit

Young professionals and couples, HDB and condo.
