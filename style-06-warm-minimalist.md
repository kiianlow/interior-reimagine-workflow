# Style 06: Warm Minimalist

## Prompt block

Warm minimalist look. Warm off-white, soft beige and taupe with light timber, travertine and natural stone accents, boucle and linen, soft curved shapes, warm lamp light, a little greenery. Refined, calm and livable, premium but understated.

## Mood

Refined, calm, livable, premium without showing off.

## Palette

Warm off-white, soft beige, warm taupe, light timber, restrained charcoal.

## Furniture

Use only the line for the room in the photo.

- Living: low curved sofa in boucle or linen, sculptural occasional chair, round travertine coffee table, textured rug, linen floor lamp, full-height sheer curtains.
- Dining: clean-lined oak table, upholstered chairs.
- Bedroom: simple upholstered bed, oak bedside tables, textured rug, full-height sheer curtains.
- Study: oak desk, upholstered chair, slim shelf, ceramic lamp.
- Kitchen: ceramic canisters, a travertine tray, a small plant.
- Bathroom: folded beige towels, a stone tray, a small plant.
- Balcony: teak lounge chair, small side table, potted olive tree.

## Textiles and window treatments

Full-height sheer curtains, neutral cushions, textured rug.

## Lighting

Warm lamps. Cove lighting only where the ceiling already has it.

## Greenery and decor

Small amounts of greenery, ceramic vases, minimal art.

## REVAMP finishes

Use this section only in REVAMP mode. In STAGE mode, keep every existing finish.

- Walls: Warm off-white or soft beige matte paint.
- Flooring: Light oak-look vinyl or large-format warm beige porcelain. Warm beige porcelain in wet areas.
- Ceiling lights: Warm pendants at existing light points. Cove lighting only where the ceiling already has it.
- Carpentry: Integrated-looking warm white or light oak built-ins.
- Kitchen: flat warm-white or light oak fronts, travertine-look or warm quartz top, fluted glass accents.
- Bathroom: warm beige large-format tiles, oak vanity, brushed nickel or brushed bronze tapware.

## Avoid

Pure white, black, gold, heavy marble, cold minimalism.

## Marketing fit

HDB upgraders, resale condos. The safest broad-appeal listing style.
